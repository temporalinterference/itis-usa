## Tissue Properties Database v5.0

The IT'IS Foundation maintains the most comprehensive database of dielectric and thermal properties of biological tissues.

### New Features in v5.0

- Extended frequency range: 1 Hz to 100 GHz
- 25 new tissue types added
- Temperature-dependent properties for key tissues
- Enhanced uncertainty quantification
- Improved data visualization tools

### Applications

The database is used worldwide by:
- Researchers modeling electromagnetic field interactions
- Medical device designers
- Regulatory agencies
- Educational institutions

### Access

The database is freely available online and integrates seamlessly with major computational electromagnetics software packages.
