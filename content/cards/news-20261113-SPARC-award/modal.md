## SPARC: A Sustainable Repository for Datasets and Tools Bridging the Body and the Brain

We are pleased to announce that IT’IS USA will be part of the continued development and operation of the [SPARC Portal](https://osparc.io), with renewed [NIH funding](https://reporter.nih.gov/search/MHoHk6xl40mbOdQto-rIkQ/project-details/11264491). As part of this effort, IT’IS USA in collaboration with IT’IS will maintain and further expand the [o²S²PARC](https://sparc.science/tools-and-resources/4LkLiH5s4FV0LVJd3htsvH) simulation platform, building on the foundation established by the IT’IS Foundation SIM-Core Team in the past 8 years (see more details {{< modal-link news-20250904-oSPARC-Year8 >}}here{{< /modal-link >}}).
