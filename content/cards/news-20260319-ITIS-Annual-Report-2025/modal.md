## We are excited to share the IT'IS Foundation’s 2025 Annual Report, with highlights of a year of research, collaboration, and progress.

This year’s report features o²S²PARC, the cloud-based, online-accessible computational platform that enables collaborative, reproducible, and FAIR (Findable, Accessible, Interoperable, and Reusable) modeling and simulations.

You will also find:

* An overview of the year's achievements
* A list of ongoing projects
* Key figures on funding and infrastructure
* A list of selected publications
* The team, including the members of the board
* A list of selected partners and sponsors
