## IT'IS Annual Report 2025

**We are excited to share the IT'IS Annual Report 2025, with highlights of a year of research, collaboration, and progress.**

This year’s report features o²S²PARC, IT'IS's cloud-based, online-accessible computational platform that enables collaborative, reproducible, and FAIR (Findable, Accessible, Interoperable, and Reusable) modeling and simulations. The annual report also includes an overview of the year’s achievements, ongoing projects, key figures on funding and infrastructure, a selection of publications, IT'IS team including the board members, and a list of partners and sponsors.
