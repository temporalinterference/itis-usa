title: Research
---
IT'IS USA, Inc. conducts cutting-edge research on the interactions of electromagnetic fields with biological systems, computational modeling, and medical applications of emerging technologies, working closely with leading research universities, medical institutions, industry partners, and regulatory agencies worldwide. IT'IS USA, together with its sister orgainization IT'IS CH, is deeply involved in the development of safety standards for exposure to electromagnetic fields for the protection of workers and the public.
