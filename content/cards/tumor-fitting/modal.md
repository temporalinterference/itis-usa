## Efficient Fourier-Based Fitting of Alternating Electric Fields

Tumor treating fields (TTFields) represent an innovative cancer treatment modality using alternating electric fields to disrupt cell division.

### Novel Approach

Our researchers have developed an advanced Fourier-based optimization algorithm that:
- Maximizes field intensity at tumor sites
- Minimizes exposure to healthy tissue
- Accounts for individual patient anatomy
- Enables real-time treatment planning

### Clinical Significance

This computational approach enables:
- Personalized treatment optimization
- Improved therapeutic efficacy
- Reduced side effects
- Better patient outcomes

### Collaboration

This work was conducted in partnership with leading medical centers and demonstrates the power of computational modeling in advancing personalized medicine.

### Publication

Results published in *IEEE Transactions on Biomedical Engineering*, 2024.
