title: Latest Updates from the TCB Council Workshop of October 21 – 23
---

October 25, 2025

IT’IS USA presents latest research in millimeter and sub-THz exposure systems.
The TCB Council workshop, a semi-annual event, was held in Baltimore, Maryland from October 20 – 23, 2025.
 
This workshop was unusual in that the typical full day of FCC presentations had to be re-organized. Due to the US government shutdown, the FCC Office of Engineering and Technology (OET) and the National Institute of Science and Technology (NIST) were unable to attend.
