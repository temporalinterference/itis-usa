IT’IS USA is promoting [o²S²PARC](https://sparc.science/tools-and-resources/4LkLiH5s4FV0LVJd3htsvH), a simulation platform created as part of the [NIH SPARC Program](https://commonfund.nih.gov/sparc) to ensure sustainable, reproducible, and FAIR computational modeling in the field of bioelectronic medicine.
