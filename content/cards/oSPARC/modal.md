## o²S²PARC

[o²S²PARC](https://sparc.science/tools-and-resources/4LkLiH5s4FV0LVJd3htsvH) is an open, cloud-based simulation platform developed by [IT’IS](https://itis.swiss/s/news-events/news/latest-news) as part of the [National Institutes of Health Common Fund SPARC Program](https://commonfund.nih.gov/sparc) to support sustainable, reproducible, and FAIR (Findable, Accessible, Interoperable, Reusable) computational modeling in bioelectronic medicine and beyond.

### Platform Overview
*	Cloud-based and web-accessible – users need only a web browser
*	Enables researchers to build, run, and share complex computational pipelines
*	Pipelines can be turned into easy-to-use, configurable applications
*	Provides graphical user interface and access to a large library of modules

### Core Capabilities
*	Transform models and code into reusable studies, workflows, and services
*	Develop and execute simulations, data analysis pipelines, and visualizations
*	Facilitate collaboration among research teams and institutions globally

### Vision and Impact
*	Promotes open, collaborative, scalable, and user-extensible computational research
*	Emphasizes FAIR principles, supporting sustainable, reproducible, and transparent research practices across disciplines
*	Rapidly growing user adoption highlights its value for global research communities
