---
title: IEC TC 106 and IEEE ICES TC34 Meetings in Mexico City
---

June 04, 2025

The [IEC TC106](https://www.iec.ch/dyn/www/f?p=103:7:0::::FSP_ORG_ID,FSP_LANG_ID:1303,25) and [IEEE ICES TC34](https://sagroups.ieee.org/ices/) joint standards meetings were held in Mexico City last week, generously hosted by the Mexican National Committee of IEC TC106. The warm weather and vibrant location at [National Polytechnic Institute](https://www.ipn.mx/english/) provided a conducive environment to present solutions and develop consensus on the next electromagnetic exposure assessment standards.
