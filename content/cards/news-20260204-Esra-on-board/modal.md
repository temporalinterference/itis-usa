## Dr. Esra Neufeld Elected to IT’IS USA Board

IT’IS USA, Inc. is pleased to announce the election of Dr. Esra Neufeld to its Board of Directors, effective February 2, 2026. Esra brings extensive experience in the field of computational life and health sciences, having led the Computational Life Sciences group at [IT’IS-CH]( https://itis.swiss/) since 2008, as well as having served as an Associate Director of the foundation since 2018. Since 2009, Esra has been the Chief Science Officer at [Zurich MedTech AG](https://zmt.swiss/), responsible for development of medical treatment planning software and medical image processing tools, and in 2019, he co-founded [TI Solutions AG](https://temporalinterference.com) to develop high-quality and flexible neuro-stimulation devices and treatment planning tools for temporal interference research. Esra has been the driving force behind the stunning success of the National Institutes of Health Common Fund project [o2S2PARC: Open platform for online simulations for stimulating peripheral activity to relieve conditions](https://osparc.io/).

Esra joins a dedicated board working to advance the missions of IT’IS USA and IT’IS CH, including to foster innovative research in neuroscience by pursuing advanced research in simulation methodologies and applications of physical, physiological, and biological processes in the field of precision medicine, and to promote academic collaboration by providing a proactive creative innovative research environment for the cultivation of sound science and good education.
