---
title: FDA Approval of Breakthrough Neuroimmune Modulation System for Rheumatoid Arthritis
---

August 20, 2025

**SetPoint Medical, supported by IT’IS Customized Research, has recently obtained FDA approval for an implanted, rechargeable neurostimulator and its accompanying neck-worn wireless charger. The system delivers electrical pulses to help reduce inflammation in patients with rheumatoid arthritis, with the IT’IS team ensuring dosimetric compliance.**
