## Efficient Cavity Resonance Prediction for MRI Safety

**Authors:** Chen, L., Anderson, P.R., Martinez, E.F., et al.

**Journal:** *IEEE Transactions on Biomedical Engineering*, Vol. 71, Issue 6, pp. 2134-2145 (2024)

**DOI:** 10.1109/TBME.2024.xxx

### Abstract

We present a novel computational method for efficiently predicting cavity resonances in patient-specific anatomical models, significantly reducing computation time while maintaining accuracy for MRI safety assessments.

### Innovation

- Machine learning-enhanced resonance prediction
- 50x faster than traditional full-wave simulation
- Maintains >95% accuracy compared to reference methods
- Enables real-time clinical decision support

### Technical Approach

- Deep learning architecture trained on 10,000+ anatomical variants
- Physics-informed neural networks
- Uncertainty quantification framework
- Validation against gold-standard numerical methods

### Applications

- Pre-scan safety assessment
- Personalized imaging protocol optimization
- Medical device compatibility screening
- Emergency imaging decision support

### Clinical Significance

Enables rapid safety evaluations that were previously impractical, potentially expanding MRI access for patients with implanted devices.
