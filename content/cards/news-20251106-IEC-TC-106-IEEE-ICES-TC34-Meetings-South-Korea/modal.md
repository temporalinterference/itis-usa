## Successful IEC TC 106 and IEEE ICES TC34 meetings in Busan, South Korea

The [IEC TC106](https://www.iec.ch/dyn/www/f?p=103:7:0::::FSP_ORG_ID,FSP_LANG_ID:1303,25) and [IEE ICES TC34](https://sagroups.ieee.org/ices/) joint standards meetings were held in Busan, South Korea last week, generously hosted by the [National Radio Research Agency](https://www.rra.go.kr/en/index.do) (RRA) and well organized by the [Telecommunications Technology Association](https://www.tta.or.kr/eng) (TTA). The relaxed beach atmosphere in Busan provided a good environment to make significant progress on key topics for human exposure assessment standards.

Two research contributions of IT’IS and IT'IS USA have been presented and discussed, both improving the efficiency of system validation of the Committee Draft of [IEC/IEEE PAS 62209-5](https://iec.ch/ords/f?p=103:14:1772202771203::::FSP_ORG_ID:50737), one by adding statistical sampling techniques, and one by performing pattern validation. The publicly available specification (PAS) is on track for publication in late 2026, after which it will be adopted into [IEC/IEEE 62209-3](https://webstore.iec.ch/en/publication/30773) and [IEC/IEEE 62209-1528](https://webstore.iec.ch/en/publication/62753) standards for specific absorption rate (SAR) measurement.

The skin model based on measurements of 44 volunteers published by IT’IS (Christ *et al*., bioRxiv 2025.05.26.652827; doi: [https://doi.org/10.1101/2025.05.26.652827](https://doi.org/10.1101/2025.05.26.652827) has been adopted as the reference skin model for measurement and simulations of absorbed power density (APD) in [IEC/IEEE 63195-3/4](https://www.iec.ch/dyn/www/f?p=103:38:11690911984084::::FSP_ORG_ID,FSP_APEX_PAGE,FSP_PROJECT_ID:1303,23,127912). These standards are scheduled for publication in late 2027. Meanwhile, a Technical Report (TR) for APD assessment [IEC/IEEE TR 63572](https://www.iec.ch/dyn/www/f?p=103:38:11690911984084::::FSP_ORG_ID,FSP_APEX_PAGE,FSP_PROJECT_ID:1303,23,122612) has concluded final voting.

[IEC/IEEE 62209-1528 Amendment 1](https://www.iec.ch/dyn/www/f?p=103:38:11690911984084::::FSP_ORG_ID,FSP_APEX_PAGE,FSP_PROJECT_ID:1303,23,121652) was recently submitted to IEEE for voting, and the parallel IEC voting process is in preparation, with publication expected in May 2026. This amendment includes important updates for SAR evaluation of devices using dynamic power control, proximity sensors, and motion sensors for exposure mitigation, as well as procedures for measurement of SAR in the hand. Niels Kuster presented a new and fast method to combine spatial sSAR1g/10g distributions from simultaneous transmitters that complies with existing procedures. These sSAR1g/10g distributions will become available before end of the year in [DASY8 Module SAR and APD](https://speag.swiss/products/dasy8/m-sar-apd/) as well as [cSAR3D](https://speag.swiss/products/csar3d/csar3d-overview/).

The week also included progress and updates on [IEC/IEEE 62704](https://webstore.iec.ch/en/publication/34411) series of numerical standards for SAR evaluation, [IEC/IEEE 63480](https://www.iec.ch/dyn/www/f?p=103:38:11690911984084::::FSP_ORG_ID,FSP_APEX_PAGE,FSP_PROJECT_ID:1303,23,112912) for radiative wireless power transfer (WPT) systems, [IEC/IEEE 63184](https://webstore.iec.ch/en/publication/62149) for inductive WPT systems, and [IEC/IEEE 62209-3](https://webstore.iec.ch/en/publication/30773) for SAR measurement by vector measurement-based systems.

Mark Douglas of IT'IS received the [1906 Award](https://www.iec.ch/awards) by the IEC in recognition of exceptional achievements in advancing IEC standards.
