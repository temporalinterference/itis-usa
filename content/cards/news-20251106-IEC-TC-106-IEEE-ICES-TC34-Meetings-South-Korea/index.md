---
title: Successful IEC TC 106 and IEEE ICES TC34 meetings in Busan, South Korea
---

November 06, 2025

The [IEC TC106](https://www.iec.ch/dyn/www/f?p=103:7:0::::FSP_ORG_ID,FSP_LANG_ID:1303,25) and [IEE ICES TC34](https://sagroups.ieee.org/ices/) joint standards meetings were held in Busan, South Korea last week, generously hosted by the [National radio Research Agency](https://www.rra.go.kr/en/index.do) (RRA) and well organized by the [Telecommunications Technology Association](https://www.tta.or.kr/eng) (TTA).
