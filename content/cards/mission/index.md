---
title: Mission
---
IT'IS USA is an independent non-profit research corporation dedicated to expanding the scientific basis of the safe and beneficial application of electromagnetic energy in health, information, and communication technologies. IT'IS USA is committed to improving and advancing the quality of life of people with disabilities through innovative research and application of emerging technologies, endeavoring to provide a proactive, creative, and innovative research environment for the cultivation of sound science, research, and education to achieve its strategic goals by stimulating productivity and pre-eminence through research programs and meaningful collaborations with academic and industrial organizations having mutual interests.
