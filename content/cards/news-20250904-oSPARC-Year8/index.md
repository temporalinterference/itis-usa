title: o^2^S^2^PARC Receives Year 8 No-Cost Extension Under NIH SPARC Program
---

September 4, 2025

The NIH SPARC program has approved a no-cost extension for the IT’IS SIM-Core team, ensuring continued activities through an extended final year of the program.

The IT’IS team is pleased to announce that our work as SPARC Modeling and Simulation (SIM-) Core and specifically on our [o^2^S^2^PARC](https://sparc.science/tools-and-resources/4LkLiH5s4FV0LVJd3htsvH) simulation platform has been granted a no-cost extension under the [NIH SPARC program](https://commonfund.nih.gov/sparc).
