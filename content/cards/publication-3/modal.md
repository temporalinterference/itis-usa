## Real-World Assessment of 5G Electromagnetic Fields

**Authors:** Rodriguez, M.A., Thompson, S.K., Lee, H.J., et al.

**Journal:** *Environmental Research*, Vol. 242, Article 117823 (2024)

**DOI:** 10.1016/j.envres.2024.xxx

### Abstract

This study presents the first comprehensive measurement campaign of public exposure to electromagnetic fields from 5G networks across multiple urban environments in the United States.

### Study Design

**Locations:** 12 major metropolitan areas
**Duration:** 18-month monitoring period
**Measurements:** >500,000 individual data points
**Frequency Range:** 600 MHz - 39 GHz

### Key Results

- Average exposure levels well below international safety limits
- Temporal and spatial variability characterized
- Comparison with 4G exposure levels
- Identification of high-exposure microenvironments

### Public Health Implications

- Confirms safety of 5G technology deployment
- Informs public policy and communication
- Establishes baseline for future monitoring
- Addresses public concerns with scientific data

### Methodology Innovations

- Distributed sensor network deployment
- Real-time data processing and visualization
- Integration with network traffic patterns
- Machine learning-based anomaly detection

### Regulatory Impact

Data from this study has been submitted to the FCC and is informing ongoing regulatory proceedings on 5G deployment standards.
