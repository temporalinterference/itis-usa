title: History
---
The Foundation for Research on Information Technologies in Society USA, Inc. (IT'IS USA), is a financially and legally independent tax-exempt institution based in Maryland, USA. 

IT’IS USA was established as a sister organization of the Foundation for Research on Information Technologies in Society (IT’IS) in Zurich, Switzerland, to foster collaboration with US research institutions, engage with US regulatory agencies, support innovation in U.S. industry, address region-specific research needs, and facilitate the formulation of safety standards in the USA.
