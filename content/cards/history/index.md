title: History
---
The Foundation for Research on Information Technologies in Society USA, Inc. (IT'IS USA), is a sister organization of the Foundation for Research on Information Technologies in Society (IT'IS CH) in Zurich, Switzerland. IT'IS USA is a financially and legally independent tax-exempt institution based in Maryland, USA. IT'IS USA was established to foster collaboration with US research institutions, engage with US regulatory agencies, support innovation in US industry, address region-specific research needs, and facilitate the formulation of safety standards in the USA.
