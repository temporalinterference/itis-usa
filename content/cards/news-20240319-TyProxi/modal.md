## TyProxi's Research Findings Lead to a Commercial Test System

**Results of the successful collaboration between IT’IS and Semtech on the project “TyProxi” has led to the development of a new module for reliable evaluation of time-averaged proximity sensors.**

Proximity sensors, which are crucial for modern mobile wireless devices like smartphones, tablets, and laptops, optimize connectivity by enabling devices to transmit at higher power when not operated close to the body, ensuring better performance while keeping electromagnetic field exposure within regulatory limits. 

For two years, IT’IS and [Semtech](https://www.semtech.com/), the market leader in proximity sensors, joined forces on collaborative TyProxi project, co-financed by Innosuisse, to develop a regulatory-approved testing system for demonstration of compliance of wireless devices with proximity sensors that can simulate real-world user conditions, including high-stress scenarios.

The project findings resulted in development of a test procedure and a prototype implementation to evaluate the trigger distance and conditions of proximity sensors. The two teams also worked closely with standardization bodies and regulators, which led to successful applications to [Canadian](https://ised-isde.canada.ca/site/certification-engineering-bureau/en/radiofrequency-energy-and-safety/ised-approved-tas-algorithms-list) and [U.S.](https://www.fcc.gov/) regulatory agencies who recently approved the Semtech PerSe® Time-Averaged Proximity Sensor (TA-PS) Time-Averaged SAR (TAS) algorithm that is implemented in their latest [PerSe® Connect](https://www.semtech.com/technology/perse) chipsets.

Importantly, the results of this work are now being put into practice and will form the basis for a new module to be released in June by IT’IS' partner SPEAG as part of their DASY product line.

Read more about the new DASY8 Module SENS on the [SPEAG press release](https://speag.swiss/news-events/news/measurement/2024/pre-announcement-new-dasy8-module-sens-for-ta-ps-performance-evaluations/).
